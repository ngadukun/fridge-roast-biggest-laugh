## The roast

> My smart fridge spent six hours updating its firmware, and now the ice maker dispenses one cube a day — proudly, like it is artisanal.

Aimed at the fridge itself — its firmware and its ice maker — not the tired
"it orders your groceries / it watches you" cracks. Single line, 134 characters.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22+, no dependencies) writes the roast to
`/output/entry.txt` — or to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when that
variable is set:

```sh
node main.ts
```
